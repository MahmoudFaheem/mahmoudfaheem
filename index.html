Mahmoud Faheem Mohamed Faheem

Pharmacist · Computational Biologist · Microbial Genomics
Investigating antimicrobial resistance dynamics through metagenomics, mobile genetic elements, and sequence analytics.
I am a university teaching assistant in the Department of Microbiology & Immunology at MTI University and currently pursuing my MSc in Pharmaceutical Sciences at Ain Shams University. My work bridges high-throughput sequence data analysis with classical antimicrobial susceptibility testing, exploring how resistance genes mobilize across bacterial populations.
Previously, I completed a Postgraduate Diploma in Genomics & Bioinformatics and a B.Pharm in Clinical Pharmacy at Cairo University, and conducted research at the Biotechnology & Cell Culture Units and Children's Cancer Hospital Egypt (57357).
